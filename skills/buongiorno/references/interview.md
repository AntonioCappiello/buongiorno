# Interview guide

Two short rounds. Round 1 gets enough to build a good first newsletter; round 2 adds the
extras, either right away (if the user is keen) or after the first email arrives, when
they know what they want to tune.

Ask 3-5 questions per message. Questions marked **(MC)** work well as multiple choice;
offer the options shown. For open questions, give an example answer so people know the
level of detail you mean. Every personal question is optional: say so, and say why you
ask. Skip what clearly doesn't apply.

## Round 1: essentials

### Who and when
- What's your first name? (For the greeting and the footer.)
- What should we call your newsletter? Default: a morning greeting in your language.
- When should it arrive? **(MC)** Every day / Weekdays only / Weekdays plus a lighter weekend edition. And at what time?
- How long should it be? **(MC)** Quick skim (~5 min) / Medium (~10 min) / Full read (~15 min).

### Places
- Where do you live? Town or city, and the neighbourhood if you like.
- Where did you grow up, and where does your family live now?
- Any small towns or villages you'd like to follow? (These are where this newsletter beats every news app.)
- Is there a company, hospital, school, factory or institution in those places that matters to someone close to you? (A dedicated section for it is often the most valued part.)
- Which language would you like each part in? For example: news from your home country in your own language, news from where you live translated into a language you read easily, everything else in English. Explain that translation is part of the service: if they live somewhere whose language they don't read well, the agent translates that news for them.

### Work
- What do you do? **(MC)** Employee / Freelancer or self-employed / Business owner / Student / At home / Retired / Prefer not to say.
- Then adapt: the field or industry; an employer, clients, products or competitors to watch; platforms or rules their work depends on (app stores, marketplaces, social networks, a regulator, a union); publications or newsletters they already like.

Don't pull the reader's own business metrics into the newsletter unless asked; many people
already have a dashboard for that.

### Hobbies and interests
- Which hobbies and sports do you do, and which do you follow (teams, leagues, artists, authors)?
- For each hobby or team: what would you like? **(MC, several allowed)** Results only / News / Tips and inspiration / Events and competitions / Best weather window / Alerts (frost, storms, closures) / Nothing. For tips: how often (daily / weekly)?
- Where do you usually do it? The weather that matters for a hike, a climb, a surf or a sail is often not at home.
- Other topics you'd enjoy: science, tech, culture, books, film, travel, food, pets, faith, local history?

### Limits and delivery
- Anything you never want to see? Celebrity news, a particular sport, graphic crime, distressing war coverage? For readers who find the news stressful, offer a calmer setup: a few short, factual world items, no graphic detail, and something good or local at the end.
- How should it reach you? **(MC)** Telegram on your phone / Email / Slack / Discord. For email: which provider do you already use? See `delivery.md`.
- Who will do the 10-minute setup at the end, you or someone helping you? Do you have a computer for it? (Some steps are hard on a phone.)

## Round 2: extras

- **Weather and alerts for family places:** should the Today block also warn about severe weather or other alerts where your family lives?
- **Health:** anything the weather block should watch, like pollen (which type?), air quality, UV or heat?
- **Getting around:** how do you travel day to day (car, bus, train, bike)? Any route or line where delays matter?
- **Family:** kids or others in your care, and their ages? Only used for events, school holidays and kids' activities.
- **Money and admin:** which country do you pay tax in, employed or self-employed, renting or owning? Any deadlines you'd like reminders for? Market numbers (exchange rates, interest rates, an index)? Always as information, never advice.
- **Events calendar:** **(MC)** No / Yes. If yes: what kinds of events, how far ahead (1-6 months), how far will you travel, show ticket prices, and how often should it come **(MC)** with every edition / twice a week / once a week?
- **Tone:** **(MC)** Neutral and factual / A little warmer and more personal.
- **Anyone else** who should get a copy (a partner, the family)? Only add people who have
  agreed to receive it, and leave the "Your actions" box and money items out of their copy.

## Always end with an open question

> "Is there anything else you'd want to know every morning that we haven't covered?
> Think of what you check on your phone first thing, or what you wish someone told you."

People often add the most personal and useful things here: a school's announcements page,
waste collection days (ask only for the pattern, e.g. "black bin Tuesdays, even weeks", never the address), a sports club's results, market days, a church calendar, the ferry
timetable, road closures on their commute. Turn each answer into a source, a section, a
static fact or a line in the Today block.

## Confirm

Play back a compact profile and get a yes before searching for sources, for example:

> **Morning, Aoife** at 07:30 Europe/Dublin, every day, ~5 min, neutral tone.
> Sections: Westport and Mayo (EN), Ireland (EN), Gdańsk (PL), World (EN, 3 items),
> Dairy farming (EN), Connacht Rugby, Sea swimming (tips + best window at Bertra beach).
> Today block: weather + tide times, Bus Éireann delays, storm warnings for Gdańsk.
> Delivery: Telegram.
